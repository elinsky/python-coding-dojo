Choose the right level and breadth of practice [@Young2018, W3]
Level:

- High
	- Natural, robust, complicated
	- Ex: Play a game of chess
	- Points out weaknesses which subsequently informs low-level practice
	- Helps you incorporate low-level skills holistically
- Low
	- Specific, simple, rote activities
	- Ex: Chess drills
    - Use to reinforce weak points

Breadth:

- Narrow
	- Doesn't cover everything
- Broad
	- Covers most areas of use
	- Higher level practice tends to be broader
	- Generally the broader the better.
	- You may need multiple practice activities to get enough breadth
