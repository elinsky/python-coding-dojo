Consciously determine and select the area of practice that will result in the most rapid skill gain [@Young2018, W3]:
 
- Emphasize what your weak spots
- Emphasize core principles over ancillary or accessory ones
- Emphasize what you will most likely use later

Practical Tips

- One approach is the shotgun method. §[[201805191125]]
- Do hard questions first
- Among hard questions, favor those with more breadth
- Take a final exam immediately after reading the book or watching the lectures
    - This will point out your weak spots
    - Don't view this as a test; view it as a learning tool and a continuation of the learning process.
