# 201805191330 Visualization plus other senses makes ideas stickier
#learning #memory
[[201805191035]] [[201805191043]] [[201809080937]]

Process:

1) Identify the concept
2) Pick a mental image
3) Is the process static or does it move through time?
4) Add another sensation
5) Add emotions if possible
6) Refine and repeat until you can bring the image up in a few seconds

Tips:

- This is generally easier to do with concrete info.  Abstract info tends to benefit more from metaphor.

## Cross-connections

[[202509061743 GTD Vision - Mental Focus Mechanism]] - This multisensory visualization technique enhances GTD's mental focus mechanism. When you add emotions and multiple senses to your vision, you strengthen the pattern-recognition programming that Allen describes, making you more likely to notice opportunities aligned with your vision.

[[202509011605 Real daydreaming technique]] - Wishcraft's real daydreaming technique applies these same principles - using present-tense, sequential, sensory-rich visualization to make visions "sticky" enough to pursue.
