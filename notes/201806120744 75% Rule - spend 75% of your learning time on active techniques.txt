# 201806120744 75% Rule - spend 75% of your learning time on active techniques. 25% on gaining insight
#learning #deliberate_practice #memory #active_recall #fluency_illusion
[[201806110744]] [[201912030745]] [[201806101539]]

Start with coverage [@Young2018, W3]:

- Reading
- Video watching

Application:

- 75% Practice - Outside of your normal input material (books, lectures, online courses) 75% of your learning time should be with active learning techniques.
	- Practice
	- Projects
	- Problems
	- Active Recall
	- Flashcards
	- Spending time rereading notes only contributes to the fluency illusion.
- 25% Intuition - The other 25% should be spent on gaining insight with the Feynman Technique and the 5 Year Old Method.
