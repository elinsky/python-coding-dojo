Construct, Model, Highway mental model for how your brain manages memory.

Construct

- Set of tightly linked knowledge
- Ex: Algebra
- Metaphor: City with buildings connected by roads.
- All the buildings (ideas or nodes) in the city are tightly connected to others via roads (links, associations, relationships)
- The more you build out a construct, the better you understand the concept

Model

- Simplified and compressed version of reality.  A simplified construct.  AKA mental model
- You use a mental model to significantly accelerate learning of a new subject or to add a significant expansion to an existing construct.
- Metaphor: Pioneers starting a new city.
- Models aren't perfect.  But they are still useful.  As you learn more, you can correct old models and build better ones.

Highway

- Relationships between constructs
- Metaphor: Highway connecting two cities
- Highways increase your creativity.
- Allows you to apply lessons across disciplines
- Ex: linking an idea in biology to one in business
