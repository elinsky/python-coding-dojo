# 201805191125 Model debugging - Practice to test out your knowledge for gaps in understanding
#learning #memory #deliberate_practice
[[201805191103]] [[201805191035]] [[201806110755]] [[201806120729]] [[201809071652]] [[201809081108]] [[201909021710]] [[201912040754]]

- You will make bad connections and misunderstand ideas.
- Debugging is the practice of fixing bad links and correcting misunderstandings.
- Metaphor: Construction crew repairing roads and buildings.  Doing maintenance work.
- How?
	- Deliberate practice!
	- Can also read contrarian opinions
	- Shotgun approach
		- Run through a wide variety of questions, not the same type over and over.
		- Check answers after each question.  Create tight feedback loops.
		- Use incorrect answers as an indicator that you need to go back to the text to correct your understanding.
		- Need to separate 'simple typo errors' from 'don't understand the concept' errors.
		- Leverages the interleaving effect
	- Apply distributed learning / the spacing effect
