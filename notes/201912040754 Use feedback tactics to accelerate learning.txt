# 201912040754 Use feedback tactics to accelerate learning
#learning
[[201805191125]]

Levels of feedback quality:

* Feedback on only the outcome (ex: applause level after a speech)
* Feedback on the behavior that produced an outcome (ex: you tell a joke and see that it flops)
* Feedback on the behavior and outcome plus a prescription for improvement.  Often this is given by a coach.

Feedback tactics:

* Metalearning - Is your overall learning strategy working?  Measure this by your learning rate over time.  Is it time to mix up the strategy?
* High intensity, rapid feedback - Find a way to get an order of magnitude MORE feedback than the average person learning your skill.  Ex: Language immersion.  Ex:  Giving 1-2 new speeches per day (even when they aren't well rehearsed yet).
* Hit the difficulty sweet spot - Good feedback should be unpredictable.  50% correct is the optimal rate for learning.
