# 201806120717 Distributed practice is second most effective learning technique
#distributed_learning #deliberate_practice #learning #memory
[[201806120800]] [[201912030745]] [[201806101539]]

Distributed practice is second most effective learning technique [@Young2018, W3].  It is the practice of spacing out your practice.  Instead of spending 5 hours on one day practicing, you would be better off spending 1 hour on 5 days practicing.

For notecards, you can use spaced repetition software to schedule your practice. §[[201806110819]]

Distributed practice isn't just limited to notecards for detail-oriented subjects.  It also works well for highly practical skills like drawing, and highly conceptual ones like mathematics or marketing.
