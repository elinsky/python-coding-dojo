# Complexity Comparison

**Q:** What are the time/space complexities for different 3-sum approaches?

**A:**

| Approach | Time | Space |
|----------|------|-------|
| Brute force (3 nested loops) | O(n³) | O(1) |
| Recursive + memoization | O(n × t) | O(t) |
| Hash-based 2-sum | O(n²) | O(n) |
| Sort + two-pointer 2-sum | O(n²) | O(1) |

O(n²) time is optimal for 3-sum.
